# Personas

## Purpose

Defines the primary user personas for the product. Each persona describes the user's role, goals, responsibilities, pain points, needs, and expected outcomes to demonstrate how product decisions are connected to specific user groups.

## Persona 1 — MSP Administrator

### Goals

- Manage multiple customer environments
- Reduce repetitive operational work
- Standardize processes
- Respond to incidents quickly

### Pain Points

- Large alert volume
- Repetitive ticket creation
- Different processes for different customers
- Limited engineering resources

### Success

"I can automate recurring operational processes without asking a developer
to build an integration."

---

## Persona 2 — IT Operations Engineer

### Goals

- Reduce alert noise
- Automate incident response
- Maintain reliable workflows

### Pain Points

- Too many repetitive alerts
- Manual investigation
- Context switching

### Success

"The system automatically handles predictable events and lets me focus
on exceptions."

---

## Persona 3 — Service Desk Manager

### Goals

- Improve SLA compliance
- Standardize ticket handling
- Measure operational performance

### Pain Points

- Manual ticket routing
- Inconsistent prioritization
- Delayed escalation
