# Customer Research

> Portfolio case study. Research findings are illustrative.

## Purpose

Documents the customer research used to understand and validate the problem. It summarizes illustrative interviews, support feedback, workflow observations, and product analytics, and identifies recurring patterns that influence product decisions.

## Research Method

Example research approach:

- 8 customer interviews
- 3 support case reviews
- 2 workflow observation sessions
- Product analytics review

## Key Findings

### Finding 1

Customers spend significant time processing repetitive alerts.

### Finding 2

Customers understand the business process but do not necessarily
understand the technical implementation.

### Finding 3

Users want automation but are concerned about incorrect automated actions.

### Finding 4

Reusable templates can reduce the initial configuration effort.

### Finding 5

Users need visibility into workflow execution and failures.

## Product Implications

The product should:

- Make common workflows easy to create
- Provide reusable templates
- Require explicit configuration for potentially destructive actions
- Provide execution history
- Make failures visible
